      A CLEARER PICTURE

      Know what you’re looking at.

      Every value has a source and a timestamp. Different instruments move at different speeds.

      PSEi & stocks

      Index points and last-traded prices. Licensed gateway quotes carry their provider’s delay and original timestamp; older quotes are marked stale.

      Mutual funds

      Daily NAVPS or NAVPU, with the actual valuation date. A previous-day valuation is separate from an intraday quote.

      ETFs

      Exchange-traded price and NAV or indicative NAV are separate values, with independent source timestamps.

      Sample mode

      All prices, changes, fund names and history are fictional. The example snapshot is dated 8 October 2026. Refreshing reloads this snapshot.

      Saved locally

      Your watchlist and connection settings stay in this browser. Cached quotes can be displayed for up to 30 minutes after a failed refresh.
